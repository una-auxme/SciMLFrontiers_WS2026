# Contributors

This repository contains the website for the workshop *SciML Frontiers: Progress, Evolution, and
Future Directions*, held on 22–23 September 2026 at the University of Augsburg.

## Workshop organisers

* [Prof. Lars Mikelsons](https://www.uni-augsburg.de/en/fakultaet/fai/informatik/prof/imech/) —
  Chair of Mechatronics, University of Augsburg
* [Prof. Michael Schlottke-Lakemper](https://www.uni-augsburg.de/en/fakultaet/mntf/math/prof/hpsc/) —
  High-Performance Scientific Computing, University of Augsburg
* [Andreas Hofmann](https://www.uni-augsburg.de/en/fakultaet/fai/informatik/prof/imech/) —
  Chair of Mechatronics, University of Augsburg

The workshop is hosted by the
[Centre for Advanced Analytics and Predictive Sciences (CAAPS)](https://www.uni-augsburg.de/en/forschung/einrichtungen/institute/caaps/)
at the University of Augsburg.
